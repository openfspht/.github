# OpenFSP

## Building open-source infrastructure for digital payments

OpenFSP is an open-source initiative focused on building secure, interoperable, and accessible infrastructure for **Payment Service Providers (FSP)** and digital financial services.

Our mission is to provide open standards, reusable components, and modern technologies that enable organizations to build reliable payment ecosystems.

## Vision

A future where financial infrastructure is open, interoperable, and accessible to everyone.

## What we build

- Payment infrastructure
- Digital wallet systems
- Identity and authentication services
- Interoperability APIs
- Compliance and reporting tools
- Developer SDKs

## Projects

🚀 **OpenFSP Core**  
Core services and shared components for the OpenFSP ecosystem.

💳 **Payment Engine**  
Payment processing and transaction management.

🔐 **Identity Service**  
Identity, authentication, and access management.

📚 **RFCs & Standards**  
Technical specifications and open standards.

## Principles

- Open source first
